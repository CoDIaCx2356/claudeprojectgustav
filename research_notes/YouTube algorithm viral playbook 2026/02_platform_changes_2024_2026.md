## 1. Recommendation and discovery changes, 2024–2026 (Home, Shorts vs. long-form, new vs. returning viewers, Trending/Explore/Charts, small channels, TV, podcasts), with a 2026 focus

### Takeaway
In 2026, discovery became more personalized and more steerable by viewers. Ask YouTube added conversational AI search, YouTube announced mood-based custom Home feeds, and viewers started receiving "AI slop" survey prompts. Discovery also became more centered on the living room. The most concrete ranking change is that originality now affects reach: on **Oct 1, 2026**, YouTube said Shorts recommendations will further prioritize original content and reduce the reach of re-uploads that add nothing. Many blogs claim a 2026 algorithm "re-weighting" where satisfaction replaced watch time, but no first-party or major-outlet source confirms it. Satisfaction surveys are a long-documented input.

### Cited Findings

**Oct 1, 2026: Shorts originality update (recommendations, not monetization)**
- On Oct 1, 2026, YouTube announced (via TeamYouTube/Creator Insider) that it is updating its recommendation systems to further prioritize original content and to reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators without anything of the uploader's own added. Coverage stresses that this is a recommendation change, not a new copyright rule. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/418466/youtube-alters-shorts-algorithm-for-originality.html?edition=144055); [WeRSM](https://wersm.com/youtube-shorts-prioritizes-original-content-over-reuploads/); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/)
- According to Creator Insider, "adding value" can mean original commentary, unique edits, or storytelling that gives a clip more context. It does **not** include "minor technical edits or template-based changes simply to alter a video." — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/); [WeRSM](https://wersm.com/youtube-shorts-prioritizes-original-content-over-reuploads/)
- Monetization of such content is still governed by the existing **reused content** policy, which already requires borrowed material to be changed significantly. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/)
- YouTube gave no start date, no recovery timeline and no channel-status screen for reduced Shorts reach. — [Mark Studios](https://www.markstudios.com/blog/youtube-shorts-original-content-update-2026); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/)
- YouTube Liaison Rene Ritchie, as quoted in coverage of the update: "If your channel was previously aggregating or re-uploading and you shift towards original content, the recommendation system will re-evaluate reach." — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/); [OutlierKit explainer for clip channels](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-original-content-update/) (secondary)

**May–Oct 2026: Ask YouTube (conversational AI search)**
- Ask YouTube was announced around **Google I/O in May 2026**, in the YouTube blog post "YouTube launches interactive search and AI video remixing for creators." — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-news-google-io-2026/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-expands-ai-creation-tools-with-gemini-omni-and-conversational-search/575310/)
- How it works: users ask complex questions and follow-ups. The system breaks each question into simpler ones, runs them across YouTube's **existing search systems**, and organizes the results. Answers combine text, Shorts, long-form videos and extracted clips. Instead of generating new videos, it jumps the viewer to **the specific moment in a creator's video** that answers the question. — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/ask-youtube-conversational-ai-search/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ask-youtube-ai-search-creators-human-content)
- Rollout: it started as a test for US Premium subscribers and then expanded to US desktop. An **Oct 12, 2026** announcement made it available to US viewers on web, mobile and TV. No global availability has been announced, and reports differ on exact timing. — [Digital Discovery](https://digital-discovery.tn/ask-youtube-mobile-tv-conversational-search-creators/); [YTViews](https://www.ytviews.in/ask-youtube-expands-in-2026-how-could-ai-search-change-creator-discovery/)
- Commentary notes that which videos get selected still depends on YouTube's search ranking, so existing ranking signals still apply. It is a discovery feature, not a replacement for search, recommendation or monetization systems. — [JXT Group](https://www.jxtgroup.com/ask-youtube-is-here-and-its-about-to-reshape-how-creators-get-found/); [Digital Discovery](https://digital-discovery.tn/ask-youtube-mobile-tv-conversational-search-creators/)
- The Next Web reports that YouTube first tried AI "slideshow" answers that viewers disliked, then built a tool that points to human-made videos. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ask-youtube-ai-search-creators-human-content) (headline-level only)

**Sept 23, 2026: Made On YouTube viewer-side discovery features**
- Google's recap: "Curate your homepage with custom feeds based on your mood, get instant recommendations with Ask YouTube, and watch structured Shorts series directly from your TV." — [Google blog recap](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-updates-2026/)
- At the time of publication, only Custom Feeds had a confirmed timeline. Several other announcements had no rollout dates or technical details. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-whats-new-made-on-youtube-2026/)

**Mar 17, 2026: viewer surveys on "AI slop"**
- YouTube began testing pop-up surveys with a small group of users. The survey asks whether a video feels like "AI slop," on a five-point scale from "Not at all" to "Extremely." Some versions ask about "low-quality AI" more broadly. It appears after a viewer watches or scrolls past a video, much like the existing "Not interested" and "Don't recommend" flows. — [gHacks, Mar 23, 2026](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/03/23/youtube-is-testing-pop-up-surveys-asking-users-to-rate-videos-as-ai-slop/); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-is-asking-users-if-videos-feel-like-ai-slop-to-flag-low-quality-content-3337295/); [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/movies/youtube-is-now-asking-viewers-if-videos-feel-like-ai-slop/)
- YouTube has not said how the responses will be used, whether for recommendations, moderation or demonetization. — [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/updates/youtube-enlists-users-to-rate-videos-as-ai-slop-in-content-quality-push); [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/03/23/youtube-is-testing-pop-up-surveys-asking-users-to-rate-videos-as-ai-slop/)
- Context from late 2025: in a Kapwing study, **104 of the first 500** videos recommended to a new test account were classified as AI slop, roughly one in five. About a third were "brainrot" content. — [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/updates/ai-slop-youtube-recommendations); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/youtube-ai-slop-surge-risks-brand-safety-viewer-trust)

**How YouTube itself describes ranking, and which claims are unverified hype**

YouTube tested a cap under which "you wouldn't be recommended a video more than eight times." After the desktop homepage redesign, attention and engagement around long-form went up. The coverage is headlined "Subscribers skip 90% of uploads in their feed, YouTube director says." — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/)

**TV / living room**
- Nielsen: YouTube reached a record **13.8% of US TV watch time in May 2026** — [Advanced Television, Jul 29, 2026](https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/07/29/nielsen-youtube-takes-13-8-of-us-tv-watch-time-in-may/). **Small channels / Hype**
- 2026 creator roundups describe Hype as available to channels with roughly **500–500,000 subscribers**. ### Inferences
- **Reach is assembled per viewer and increasingly steered by the viewer.** Recommendations are "pulled" for each viewer. Custom mood feeds, Ask YouTube and the negative-feedback and slop prompts give viewers more explicit control. Gaming metadata or posting cadence matters less than whether specific viewers are satisfied and come back.
- **Originality is now a stated reach input for Shorts.** Clip, compilation and re-upload Shorts channels face lower reach (Oct 2026) on top of YPP risk (Section 4). Channels can recover by shifting to original contributions, but YouTube gave no timeline, so expect a lag.
- **Ask YouTube rewards answer-shaped moments.** It jumps viewers to the exact moment that answers a question, so clear spoken answers, chapters and descriptive titles plausibly improve the odds of being surfaced. It is US-only for now, and the underlying search ranking still decides.
- **TV is YouTube's growth surface.** Record Nielsen share, 200M+ living-room hours a day in the US and podcasts on TV all point the same way. Long-form, lean-back, episodic and podcast formats fit where YouTube is steering both viewers and advertisers.
- **Subscribers are not distribution.** If subscribers skip about 90% of uploads in their feed, each video has to earn Browse and Suggested traffic on its own merits. Subscriber count is a weak predictor of a new upload's reach.
- **Treat any "2026 algorithm update" claim with numeric weights or a precise "satisfaction replaced watch time in [month]" date as unverified marketing.** The durable, documented signals are satisfaction (surveys), viewers returning, and watch time.
- Because the Trending page is gone (see Gaps; July 2025, unverified in this session), "going viral" now means a chain of personalized recommendations rather than a single public chart placement. Category charts survive mainly for music, podcasts and trailers.

### Gaps
- I could not read TeamYouTube's Oct 1, 2026 post or the Creator Insider video directly. Whether the originality change has fully rolled out, and how originality is detected (matching vs. classifiers), is undisclosed.
- There is no official detail on how "custom feeds" work or when they launch beyond the Made On recap, so their effect on Home traffic is unknown.
- **UNVERIFIED (background knowledge, high confidence):** On **July 21, 2025**, YouTube announced it would retire the **Trending page and "Trending Now" list** over the following weeks. It cited sharply declining visits and the shift to personalized recommendations. Replacements were category charts on YouTube Charts (e.g., Trending Music Videos, Weekly Top Podcast Shows, Trending Movie Trailers), the Explore menu's category pages, and trend/inspiration views for creators in the Studio Inspiration tab. An official URL is still needed.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, high confidence):** In his **Feb 2025** annual letter, Mohan said TV had overtaken mobile as the primary device for watching YouTube in the US.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium confidence):** In 2025, YouTube experimented with a prompt-based "Your custom feed" on Home. It is likely the precursor to the Sept 2026 custom feeds.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium confidence):** YouTube staff (Beaupré, Creator Insider) said in 2024–2025 that Shorts and long-form are recommended largely independently, and that posting Shorts doesn't hurt a channel's long-form recommendations. No official 2026 page was retrieved.
- No 2026 changes to the Explore menu were found. No official 2024–2026 program specifically boosting small channels was found besides Hype.
- No official statement on how new vs. returning viewers are weighted was retrieved (see Section 2 for the analytics segments).

## 2. New creator tools that affect growth (Test & Compare, Hype, Inspiration/Ask Studio, dubbing, Collaborations, Communities, Analytics, Shorts/AI tools, likeness detection) and reported results

### Takeaway
The tool shift that matters most for growth is **built-in packaging experiments judged on watch time**. Test & Compare now covers **titles as well as thumbnails**, globally since Dec 2025. At Made On 2026, YouTube announced **audience-specific multi-thumbnails** and **A/B tests of video cuts**. Around that, YouTube is filling Studio and Shorts with Gemini/Veo AI features (more than 1M channels used its AI tools daily in Dec 2025), plus series formats, live and dubbing features, and wider likeness protection. YouTube publishes no benchmark results, and third-party "lift" figures are unverified.

### Cited Findings

**Test & Compare: titles and thumbnails**
- Title A/B testing rolled out globally to all creators with advanced features in **December 2025**, inside the existing Test & Compare tool. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/); [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/youtube-rolls-out-a-b-testing-for-titles-to-all-creators)
- Creators can test up to **three titles, three thumbnails, or title–thumbnail combinations** on a single video. It is desktop only, and one source says there is no YPP requirement. — [YouTube Help: A/B test titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en-GB); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/); [ThumbnailTest](https://thumbnailtest.com/news/youtube-releases-title-test-compare/)
- Tests run for up to two weeks. Per YouTube Help: "the option with the highest watch time will be shown to all viewers. If there was no clear 'winner', then the first title or combination of title and thumbnail that you uploaded will be shown to all viewers." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en-GB)
- YouTube optimizes for watch time (described as **"watch time share"**) rather than CTR. Its stated reasoning is that watch time better reflects long-term success and discourages clickbait. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/); [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtubes-new-title-ab-testing-tool-everything-creators-need-to-know)
- Reported results: third-party claims of 15–30% CTR gains, and of tests needing 1,000–5,000 impressions per variant, are **not YouTube figures**. YouTube has published no official benchmarks. — [Hooksnap](https://www.hooksnap.io/blog/youtube-title-ab-testing-how-to-test-titles-thumbnails-2026); [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtubes-new-title-ab-testing-tool-everything-creators-need-to-know)

- **More than 1 million channels** used YouTube's AI creation tools daily in December 2025. — [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/youtube-channels-using-ai-tools-reduce-slop-neal-mohan-letter-1236636548/)

**Commerce and brand tools that add distribution**
- **Brandcast (May 2026):**
  - The **"Affiliate Partnership Boost"** gives more exposure to creator videos that link to products, "to drive incremental affiliate sales and creator earnings from YouTube Shopping commission."

### Inferences
- **Packaging should be tested continuously and judged on watch time share, not CTR.** Once audience-specific thumbnails ship, the "one best thumbnail" mindset gives way to designing distinct variants for distinct audiences, such as core fans vs. cold viewers. Because winners are picked on watch time, a high-CTR variant that loses viewers fast will lose the test, so clickbait defeats itself.
- **AI makes production speed a commodity.** With more than 1M channels a day using YouTube's AI tools, lasting advantage moves to originality, personality, likeness and trust. YouTube is both protecting those (likeness detection) and rewarding them (originality ranking).
- **Shorts series, Live Showdown and live/auto dubbing point to the next growth levers:** retention, returning viewers and international reach, rather than one-off spikes.
- **Paid boosts are a new distribution route.** Affiliate Partnership Boost and Creator Partnerships let brand money amplify creators' shopping-linked videos and Shorts, which does not depend on organic recommendation.

- **UNVERIFIED (background, high confidence):** **Made on YouTube 2025 (Sept 16, 2025)** announced title testing for Test & Compare, Veo 3 (Fast) in Shorts, "Edit with AI," Ask Studio, expansion of likeness detection to YPP creators, lip-sync for auto-dubbing, and a **Collaborations** feature that credits multiple creators on one video.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, high confidence):** **Hype** was introduced at Made on YouTube 2024 (Sept 2024) as a test in a few markets for channels under 500K subscribers. It has a weekly leaderboard and a points bonus for smaller channels, and expanded broadly in 2025.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium-high confidence):**
  - **Auto-dubbing** launched in **Dec 2024** for YPP channels focused on knowledge and information content, and expanded across YPP in 2025.
  - YouTube has said creators using multi-language audio tracks get more than 25% of watch time from viewers outside the video's primary language. This was a 2023–2024 claim; the exact figure and date need checking.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium-high confidence):** **Likeness detection** was piloted with CAA-represented talent in Dec 2024 and reached the first wave of YPP creators around **Oct 2025**.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium confidence):**
  - **Communities**, channel-specific fan spaces, were announced Sept 2024 and expanded in 2025.
  - The Studio **Inspiration tab** added Gemini brainstorming in 2024.
  - **Ask Studio** arrived in 2025.
- **UNVERIFIED (background, medium confidence):** YouTube Analytics added audience segments for **new, casual and regular viewers** in 2025, replacing the simple new/returning split. Date and exact definitions need verification.
- This session found no quantitative creator-reported results for Hype, auto-dubbing, Collaborations or Communities.

## 3. Changes to view counting, Shorts length and format, and Shorts–long-form integration

### Takeaway
The main metric change in the window is the **March 2025** switch to counting every Shorts play or replay as a view. The date is from background knowledge, while 2026 sources confirm the mechanics; "engaged views" remain the YPP and revenue measure. 2026 brought changes to formats rather than metrics: **Shorts series** (seasons and episodes, including on TV), **2x playback**, planned **image posts** in the Shorts feed, **AI-likeness Shorts**, and **parental limits on Shorts**. ## 4. Monetization and content-policy changes that shape "viral" strategies, and who got hit

### Takeaway
From July 2025 to October 2026, YouTube escalated against mass-produced and low-effort content on three levels:

1. **Monetization:** the YPP "inauthentic content" policy, July 2025.
2. **Enforcement:** the reported removal in Jan 2026 of 16 large AI-slop channels with about 4.7B views, framed as spam and deceptive-practices violations.
3. **Distribution:** the Mar 2026 slop surveys and the Oct 2026 originality ranking for Shorts.

At the same time, YouTube **relaxed ad rules** in 2026 for gaming, news and public-interest content, and reportedly some sensitive topics. The losers are templated AI and faceless content farms and re-uploaders, not the use of AI as such.

### Inferences
**What this means for a creator's growth strategy now (October 2026).** These are inferences drawn from the changes above.

1. **Make your human contribution obvious early in every video, especially Shorts** (Oct 2026 ranking; Jul 2025 YPP policy; Jan 2026 removals; Mar 2026 slop surveys). That contribution can be commentary, original footage, narrative, expertise, or your face and voice. Clip and compilation channels should rebuild around commentary or analysis that adds context. Template edits do not count, by YouTube's own wording.
2. **Package for watch time share, not CTR.** Run Test & Compare on every important upload, testing titles and thumbnails together. Prepare distinct thumbnail variants for different audiences ahead of YouTube's multi-thumbnail personalization, and plan to test video cuts once that ships. Don't trust third-party "+30% CTR" claims.
3. **Design for viewer-steered and AI-mediated discovery.** Make topics and intents easy to name: clear titles, chapters, and explicit spoken answers. Ask YouTube jumps to moments, and custom feeds and surveys reward satisfaction. Negative feedback ("Don't recommend this channel," slop surveys) is now a more visible risk for low-effort content.
4. **Build for the TV.** That means long-form, lean-back and episodic formats: seasons, recurring shows, video podcasts, and Shorts series that feed into long-form. YouTube's leadership, ad sales (Brandcast) and product (Shorts series on TV, Ask YouTube on TV) all point there.
5. **Measure Shorts with engaged views and retention, not raw views.** Since 2025, raw views count plays. Treat old benchmarks and "viral Shorts" screenshots with suspicion.
6. **Don't rely on subscribers to deliver views.** Subscribers skip about 90% of uploads in their feed, so every upload must earn Browse and Suggested reach. Returning viewers come from series, consistency and satisfaction, not from the subscriber count.
7. **Use AI as an accelerator, not a substitute.** Use it for editing, dubbing, ideation and thumbnails. Disclose realistic synthetic media, and protect your likeness (detection now covers mobile, with voice detection coming).
8. **Use the 2026 ad-rule loosening** if you work in gaming, news and commentary, or educational sensitive topics. Avoid shock bait involving young people, and avoid edgy content in kids' categories.
9. **Treat commerce as a distribution lever.** Affiliate Partnership Boost, Affiliate Campaigns, Open Call and Ask Studio brand proposals can add paid amplification and revenue that don't depend on organic virality.
10. **Ignore outdated "hacks":**
    - "Get on Trending" (the page is gone; see Gaps).
    - "CTR is everything."
    - "60-second Shorts."
    - "Re-upload or compile for Shorts reach."
    - "Mass-produce faceless AI videos."
    - "Secret signal-weight tables" or "satisfaction replaced watch time in [month] 2026" (no first-party or major-outlet source).

**Archetype risk snapshot (inference):**
- **Highest risk:** re-upload, clip and compilation Shorts channels; templated AI "slop" channels, including fake trailers, AI true-crime, AI "facts" and AI personas on finance or health topics.
- **Medium risk:** faceless channels made by humans, which need visible authorship.
- **Lowest risk / tailwinds:** personality-led long-form, series and podcast creators; educational and commentary channels; gaming creators; creators building international reach with dubbing.
